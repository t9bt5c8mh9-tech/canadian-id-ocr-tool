Image2PSD

Convert PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or BMP images into Photoshop PSD files — entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Layers in the exported PSD

  • One editable raster layer containing your image
  • A flattened composite for maximum compatibility

Fonts

Not applicable — this tool converts a whole image. Text inside the image is baked into the pixels, not stored as editable text.

Layout

The PSD keeps the image's original dimensions and pixels, 8 bits per channel, RGB colour. Transparency is preserved unless you pick a background colour below.

Auto-correction options

Runs 100% client-side. Output is a PSD v1 file (8-bit RGB/RGBA, PackBits RLE) compatible with Photoshop, GIMP, Photopea and most editors.
